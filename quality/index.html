    Quality targets and evidence

    Agentmux's next quality improvements focus on a reliable first result, maintainable controls and measured outcomes. Numerical ratings are judgments; these acceptance criteria make the evidence behind a future rating reviewable. An implemented feature or passing fixture does not earn a maturity or productivity score by itself.

    Area Evidence required before arguing for 9/10
    Technical capability The primary released workflow works from installation through inspected application; supported provider routes have recorded native task and continuation checks with independent artifact validation. Unsupported routes are clear.
    Safety design and assurance Boundary regressions pass with actual isolation; an independent adversarial review covers credentials, authorization, source application and recovery; material findings are resolved and retested.
    Regression testing Linux and macOS installed-artifact first runs pass; failure controls prove that wrong output, failed actors, timeouts, stale evidence and interrupted publication cannot become success; conditional provider/resource coverage is recorded explicitly.
    Maintainability Public entry points have focused responsibilities; changes to workflow state and authority have documented invariants and targeted regression coverage; another contributor can implement and review a representative change without maintainer intervention. File count alone is not a score.
    Onboarding and usability At least five first-time participants from the intended power-user audience install the release and complete the first-result journey unaided; at least four finish within five minutes after prerequisites, with no unsafe source mutation. Also observe resume, launch, delegation, inspection and recovery in daily use with declared timing/error criteria. Record failures, experience and assistance; repeat after fixing blockers.
    Operational maturity Record at least seven days of realistic supervised use on supported environments, including provider interruption, credential refresh, restart and storage growth. Preserve task outcomes and unresolved incidents; a fast fault soak does not substitute for elapsed operation.
    Productivity advantage Run the matched evaluation against a declared single-agent baseline and a relevant public alternative; retain failures, costs where actually available, blind artifact grading and uncertainty. Require a repeatable useful improvement with acceptable quality and overhead. A small pilot cannot establish a general advantage.

    The first-result command and offline comparison controls validate infrastructure. They do not represent new-user observations, native model quality or a comparative benchmark. Keep real evaluation evidence private until its contents have been reviewed for publication.

    Recorded engineering evidence

    The bounded command-worker soak records 60 verified turns, 61 audited tasks, 12 clean restarts and an in-flight broker crash. The interrupted attempt remained uncertain, its process stopped, and the owner resolved it as failed without automatic replay. Completed traces were verified after recovery. This took 66.66 seconds, made zero model calls, and used disposable fixtures. It is not evidence of seven days of native use.

    The observability regressions cover large retained histories, complete counts, fair bounded attention and additive metadata-index migration. Query-plan checks reject reads of task bodies during operational counting/ranking. A separate read-only connection keeps slow queries and cancellation outside the worker's unchanged 500 ms lease monitor. Tests block the actual read path, retain the 250 ms query budget and check credential rotation and shutdown before publication. Worker check-ins remain separate from native events; unknown reporting never becomes a successful task outcome.

    Agentmux v0.14.0 adds fixed diagnostic codes and local collection timings to schema-1 observations. The offline operational report summarizes sampled availability, gaps, retained-state/capacity gauges and storage changes, while preserving missing evidence as unknown. It omits source paths, task identities and raw errors, and records the complete input hash. Regression controls cover malformed captures, privacy and bounded memory; an optional actual-CLI integration exercises a live command-only task and a partial observation after shutdown. This prepares operational evidence collection; it does not supply seven days of native use or unaided user results.

    The comparison summary keeps failures, incomplete/unstarted attempts and paired outcomes visible. Its timing summaries do not infer a winner, model quality or unknown provider usage. Deterministic fixture results validate that reporting route, rather than establishing a competitive advantage.

    Fresh AI peer reviews added reproducible controls for escaped secret publication, ambiguous signed trace envelopes, concurrent trace changes and cancellation races. These are scoped code reviews and regressions, not an external human security audit. The subscription pilot has frozen contract graders and route guards; preparation and metadata checks alone are not measured model or competitor outcomes.

    A subscription-route smoke completed a newly authored Go exercise in 86.541 seconds using Codex 0.166.0 with ChatGPT authentication and Claude Code 2.1.317 with first-party Pro subscription authentication. Four native CLI sessions ran. Controller checks and the frozen external grader passed; the confirmed patch applied only to the fixture and independently reproduced its final files. Observed process cleanup was clean. The record identifies the source build, rather than claiming that a later release archive was used.

    Both model reviews approved without findings and reported not running tests. Their approvals are separate from controller and external test evidence. The preceding duplicate-flag setup failure remains recorded. The workflow's sonnet profile was overridden by the pinned claude-sonnet-5-5 request; the provider's resolved model and inference-request count remain unknown. This small route check establishes no comparative productivity or long-term reliability result.

    Guided work in v0.14.0 addresses the redundant-turn case identified by that smoke. After independently passing implementation checks, an initial fresh-context approval of the exact same revision can skip the response and second-review phases. Retained reports mark those phases skipped with their reason, rather than recording imaginary model turns. Final acceptance checks still run, and source/result freshness plus explicit application guards remain. With one implementation attempt, the clean path uses two native CLI turns; retries increase that count and requested changes keep the full review loop. Legacy plans without the declared policy retain their previous behavior. Model-free controls establish those branches and rejection behavior.

    A November 4 subscription smoke exercised that clean path in 69.093 seconds with exactly two native CLI sessions: Codex implementation and Claude review. The original approval, both skipped phases, final checks, full retained-report load, external grader and confirmed fixture-only application were verified. Its generic final files and grader contract reproduce without models. This used the clean bf3a578 source build labeled 0.14.0-pre, not a published release archive. The separate November 3 and November 4 records establish no speedup or competitive advantage.

    A private exploratory subscription pilot ran three selected historical repository tasks once with a native Codex baseline and once through the actual published v0.14.0 Codex/Claude workflow. The baseline passed 3/3, while Agentmux passed 1/3. Two Agentmux implementations hit an implicit 180-second profile limit before review/application, despite a longer overall deadline. The outer guard separately rejected observed background processes and cleaned its tracked identities. These are undelivered-workflow failures, not correctness scores for completed patches.

    The first four admissions were invalidated by a cumulative process-tracker limit and are retained separately. The corrected frozen study used seven native CLI admissions, with eleven across both studies. Grader/artifact integrity and the independent evidence audit passed. Raw source, logs and local paths remain private; the linked aggregate contains only reviewed metadata. Unequal workflow and turn allowances, one repeat, selected historical tasks, trusted-local access and unknown served-model revisions prevent a general speed, quality or competitive claim.

    The v0.15.0 budget and bounded process-diagnostic changes address the concrete engineering findings. A separate regression record covers only the two failed cases: both passed 2/2 using the clean 0.15.0-rc1 source build, not a published binary. Fresh review, mandatory final checks, the same frozen external graders and fixture-only application passed. One extra package race-test command hit four native-sandbox Unix-socket permission errors; focused checks and the frozen contracts passed. This is not a full-suite success claim. The original native 3/3 versus Agentmux 1/3 remains unchanged, with no baseline rerun or pooled pass rate. Requested model aliases still do not verify served revisions, and these targeted rechecks establish no speedup or competitive advantage.

    Use workflow validation for engineering checks, evaluation for usability and comparative task studies, and release verification for the bytes delivered to users.
